Common questions

An episode says “Processing”. How long does it take?
Preparing an episode for shadowing means transcribing it and aligning every sentence to the audio, which runs on our servers rather than on your phone. It usually takes a few minutes. You can leave the screen — the episode turns “Ready” on its own.
Why can’t I prepare some shows for shadowing?
Some publishers allow their episodes to be played but not transcribed. Those shows are marked listen-only: you can listen, but the sentence-by-sentence practice is unavailable. A show can also be removed entirely at its publisher’s request.
Where do my voice recordings go?
Nowhere. Recordings you make while shadowing stay on your phone as temporary files and are deleted when you leave the sentence, or when the next day’s challenge set replaces them. They are never uploaded to us.
Do I need an account?
Yes, for almost everything. Signed out, you get one sample episode bundled inside the app — the full shadowing loop, offline, on that one episode — so you can try the practice before signing up for anything. Adding shows, the Notebook, Downloads, and the daily challenge all need an account, which is Sign in with Apple and nothing else.
How do I manage or cancel a subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us. Manage or cancel yours in the iPhone’s Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
How do I delete my account?
In the app: More → Account → Delete account. Your Notebook and library are removed from your account and can’t be recovered. Downloads already on your phone stay until you delete them.

For publishers

If you publish a podcast and want it removed from Echo-Ooh, email us and we will take it down. Our DMCA designated agent is registered with the U.S. Copyright Office under DMCA-1078370.
